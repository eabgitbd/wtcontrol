A personal reminder app that shows your messages on screen at configurable intervals — like an always-on spiritual companion.

📱 Install on Android

  1. 1Open this page in Chrome on your Android device
  2. 2Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right corner)
  3. 3Select "Add to Home screen"
  4. 4Tap "Install" — the app icon appears on your home screen
  5. 5Open from home screen for the full-screen experience

🍎 Install on iPhone / iPad

  1. 1Open this page in Safari
  2. 2Tap the Share button (box with arrow, bottom bar)
  3. 3Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4Tap "Add" — the icon appears on your home screen
